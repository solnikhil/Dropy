# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Dropy to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity. Contributors and maintainers pledge to make taking part a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent, and critique ideas rather than people.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully, and give it in the same spirit.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people who use it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments of any kind.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission. This includes screenshots or recordings that show another person's screen content.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and whenever someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@solnikhil](https://github.com/solnikhil), privately. If the problem involves the maintainer, you can [report the content to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Reports are handled confidentially.

Depending on what happened, the maintainer may respond with a private warning, remove the content, or ban someone temporarily or permanently from the project's spaces.
